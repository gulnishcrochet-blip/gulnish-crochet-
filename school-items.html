Questions about School Items

How long will my order take?

Each school items piece is stitched by hand after you order, which takes about 5 days. Delivery across Pakistan then takes about 3 days, so most orders arrive in around 8 days in total. We send you a confirmed date on WhatsApp before stitching begins.

Can I get it in another colour or size?

Yes — every school items piece can be made in the colours and size you want. Send us a photo or a description on WhatsApp and we will quote it before you commit.

Do you deliver across Pakistan?

Yes — we deliver to every city in Pakistan. The delivery charge is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay anything.

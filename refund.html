After Your Order

Refund & Exchange Policy

1. Made-to-order craftsmanship

Every piece is crocheted by hand, one at a time. Because it is handmade, a piece may differ very slightly from the photo (a different yarn batch shade, or a slightly different tension). We always send you a photo of your finished piece on WhatsApp before dispatch.

2. Defective or wrong item

If your order arrives damaged, stained, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 3 days of delivery with a photo on WhatsApp at 0307 5729901 quoting your order number. We will repair, exchange, or refund it.

3. Change of mind

Because our pieces are made to order, we cannot accept returns for a simple change of mind on custom or made-to-order items. For ready-made (in-stock) items, you may exchange within 3 days of delivery provided the item is unused, undamaged and in its original packaging.

4. Exchanges & return shipping

Exchange items are covered by us if the return is because of our mistake. For genuine exchanges on ready-made items, the return shipping cost is the customer's responsibility, and the exchange is subject to stock availability.

5. Refunds

Approved refunds are returned by bank transfer to your account within 3–5 working days, whether you paid by bank transfer, JazzCash or EasyPaisa.

6. How to start a refund or exchange

Message your order number plus a photo on WhatsApp at 0307 5729901. We reply within 24 hours during shop hours (9:00 AM – 10:00 PM).

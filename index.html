FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything shoppers ask us most. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

How long does delivery take?
Ready-to-ship pieces usually arrive within 2–4 working days. Made-to-order and custom pieces take 4–10 days depending on size and complexity — we confirm the estimate before you pay. You’ll get a tracking link by WhatsApp as soon as the parcel is dispatched.
Can I pay when the parcel arrives?
No — we don’t offer cash on delivery, since every piece is made to order. All orders are paid in advance by bank transfer, JazzCash or EasyPaisa. Message us and we’ll send the details.
Can I request a custom design?
That’s the part we love most. Send a photo or a description with your colours, size and budget on the contact page or on WhatsApp, and we’ll come back with a design, a yarn quote and a timeline. There is no charge for the first concept.
Can I change the colours or size?
Yes, on anything that hasn’t been stitched yet. Message us as soon as you place the order and we’ll check what’s still possible. Sizes and colours listed on a product page are the ones we can usually produce without extra time.
How do I track my order?
Use the Track Order page with your order number and the phone number you ordered with. You can also just ask us on WhatsApp — we’ll check the courier status for you.
What if something arrives damaged?
Send us a photo on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery. If the damage happened in transit we’ll remake and resend it at our cost, or refund you in full. Our full terms are on the Refund & Exchange Policy.
